The Heritage

An old-world gesture,
tied in gold.

Long before it was a trend, the hand chain — a ring bridged to a bracelet by a fine link of gold — moved through Anatolian courts and Ottoman households as a quiet signal of devotion, protection, and status.

DZA Jewelry studies that lineage closely, then redraws it: sharper lines, lighter settings, new stone pairings — so the piece still feels ancestral, but sits on your hand like it was made this morning.

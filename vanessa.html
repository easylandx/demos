About Vanessa

Every finish line has a story, and Vanessa has been writing hers one mile at a time. What began as a personal training diary has become a public record of discipline: 27 marathons, a growing list of ultramarathons, and the daily miles logged on the bike between race days.

Vanessa races and trains across both disciplines — running and cycling — sharing the honest, unfiltered process behind endurance sport: the early alarms, the long climbs, the recovery days, and the race-day nerves that never fully go away no matter how many bibs you've pinned on.

As an ambassador for Threshold Experts, she brings that same discipline to every partnership: consistent, credible, and genuinely lived-in content for brands that serve endurance athletes.
